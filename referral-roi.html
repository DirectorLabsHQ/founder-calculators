How This Referral ROI Calculator Works

A referral program replaces some of your paid customers with referred ones. Each referred customer costs you the referrer reward plus the discount you give the new customer. If that total is lower than your paid CAC, every referred customer lowers your blended CAC.

Referral cost = Referrer reward + Referee discount
Blended CAC = (1 − referral share) × Paid CAC + referral share × Referral cost
Return on rewards = (Paid CAC − Referral cost) ÷ Referral cost
Monthly saving = New customers × Referral share × (Paid CAC − Referral cost)

Worked Example

Paid CAC is $200. You give a $25 reward to the referrer and a $25 discount to the new customer, so a referred customer costs $50. Ten percent of your 100 monthly new customers come from referrals.

  1. Blended CAC = 0.90 × $200 + 0.10 × $50 = $185, which is 7.5% below $200.
  2. Each referred customer saves $200 − $50 = $150.
  3. Return on rewards = $150 ÷ $50 = 300%. Each $1 of rewards replaces $4 of ad spend.
  4. Monthly saving = 100 × 10% × $150 = $1,500.

What This Calculator Leaves Out

  • Incrementality: some referred customers would have signed up anyway, so you may be paying rewards for customers you already had.
  • Program costs: referral software, support time and fraud checks add cost beyond the rewards.
  • Customer value: if referred customers stay longer, your real return is higher. Test it with the CAC to LTV calculator and the guide on interpreting LTV:CAC.
  • The discount is lost revenue, not cash out: it is counted here as a cost, which is the cautious way to treat it.

Referral ROI FAQ

What is a good ROI for a referral program?

Because referral rewards are usually far smaller than paid CAC, returns above 100% are common on paper. The better test is whether blended CAC falls and whether referred customers stay at least as long as paid ones.

What are double-sided rewards?

Both the referrer and the new customer receive something. Vendors report that this lifts participation compared with rewarding only one side, but results vary by product and audience.

How is blended CAC different from paid CAC?

Paid CAC covers only paid channels. Blended CAC averages the cost across every new customer, including cheaper referral and organic ones. See the CAC payback calculator to check how fast you earn it back.
